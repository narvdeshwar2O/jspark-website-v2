The Sovereign AI Operating System for missions that cannot fail. Built in India. Deployed at national scale.

## Core Platform
JSPARK AI builds the intelligence infrastructure for nation-scale operations. Our platform handles missions in Homeland Security, Disaster Management, Critical Infrastructure, Energy, and Defence.

## Products
1. **OpsUnity AI (Foundation Platform)**: The sovereign foundation. Agent swarms, quant engines, mathematical models, and language models with no token limits and no external calls. Every JSPARK product runs on it.
2. **OpsMind**: Sovereign Document Intelligence. Reads engineering drawings, schematics, and archives at the level of a domain engineer. Queried like a conversation. Every answer traced to its source.
3. **OpsVision**: Sovereign Operational Intelligence. One living model of your entire operation, every asset, event, signal, and record fused into a single intelligence picture.
4. **OpsUnity Hydra**: AI Hydrology Model. Forecasts rainfall, floods, flash floods, and rainfall-induced landslides at basin level, hours before impact. Mapped over 3 million rivers and streams. Verified by the India Meteorological Department.

- **Uttar Pradesh 112**: The world's largest emergency response system. 75 districts, 24 crore citizens. OpsVision placed response vehicles before the call came in and corrected every misrouted call at intake (dropped from 23% to 4%). 46% faster response.
