# Project Context

## Purpose
The JSPARK Website is the frontend marketing and landing experience for JSPARK AI. It showcases highly secure, air-gapped sovereign AI operating systems primarily aimed at Homeland Security, Defense, Disaster Management, and Critical Infrastructure.

## Aesthetic
- **Vibe**: Tactical, highly secure, modern, dark enterprise.
- **Visuals**: Dark themes, sharp edges, glowing accents (primarily orange `#FF5722`), monospace sub-text, and sophisticated 3D / animation sequences.

## Domains (Features)
- `marketing`: Primary landing pages, product showcases, industry verticals, and contact forms.
- `hydra`: Specific specialized feature modules (if applicable).
